# Time wasting measurement

In parliaments politicians are discussing a lot of subjects, conducting arguments very inefficiently.
The goal of of this project is to quantify time wasting in taxpayer money.
The salary of members of the parliament is public information, it can be used as a basis for translating parliament time to.

## Examples of Time wasting

- someone in a hot-chair is being asked questions - and avoids giving clear answers
- whenever someone gets word, they often say "thank you" / "thank you to the chairman" / ... (superfluous courtesy)
- whenever someone says irrelevant things regarding a topic, the chair has to silence them
- when politician are using non-factual statements as arguments (hard and expensive to fact check)
- the intent of questions could be serving some other purpose than the public good (fx. building up some scandal case on some other party/politician - hidden intent detection problem)
- when nothing comes out of a conversation (should it be labeled wasteful?)
